Headline: Why timing matters in clinical research
Body: Average outcomes can hide critical differences in when events happen. Survival analysis helps teams understand censoring, retention, and hazard over time.

Headline: Match the model to the question
Body: Logistic regression, Poisson regression, and mixed-effects models answer different health questions. Strong analysis starts with choosing the right structure for the outcome.

Headline: Routine data can do more than report totals
